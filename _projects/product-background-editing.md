I built a workflow to replace a product photo's background and checked whether the edit preserved packaging text, logos, and edges while matching the requested scene.

## Experiment

Four catalog products received red and blue studio background edits. Across the four held-out requests, foreground conditioning increased background color coverage from 64.57% to 71.47%. Mean foreground error rose from 10.12 to 10.46 on a 0–255 scale. Hard compositing kept the masked foreground pixels unchanged.
