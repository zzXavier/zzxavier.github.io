**Accepted to ACCV 2026.** † denotes equal contribution.

MFilter addresses keypoint matchability for sparse feature matching — estimating which keypoints in an image pair can be reliably matched — while remaining lightweight. The method conditions matchability prediction on the specific image pair, rather than treating keypoints independently of the pair they are matched within.
